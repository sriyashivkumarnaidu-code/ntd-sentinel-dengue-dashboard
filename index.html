The problem
PROBLEM 01
Fragmented information
Outbreak data scattered across WHO portals, government bulletins, NGO reports and local newspapers — never in one place.
PROBLEM 02
Language barrier
Ground reality in South India is reported first in Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam. Global dashboards only track English sources.
PROBLEM 03
No early warning
Decision-makers learn of outbreaks after official reporting — often weeks too late for effective preventive action.
Our differentiators
DIFFERENTIATOR 01
Regional language monitoring
Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam and Marathi newspapers scraped and auto-translated daily. No existing public health tool does this.
DIFFERENTIATOR 02
Predictive risk scoring
Media signals combined with rainfall, temperature and historical burden produce a district-level risk score — updated daily.
DIFFERENTIATOR 03
Fully automated pipeline
Runs at 8am daily. Collects, translates, extracts and scores — pushing structured intelligence to a live database automatically.
How it works
01
Data capture
NewsData.io and four regional scrapers collect articles in English, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam and Marathi every morning.
02
AI extraction
Claude API reads each article and extracts state, district, event type, severity and a policy insight — automatically.
03
Risk scoring
News signal + environmental data + historical case burden = district risk score 0–100, updated every day.
04
Intelligence output
Decision-ready dashboard — district risk map, translated article feed, predictive alerts for policymakers.

Most tools tell you where dengue is.
NTD Sentinel tells you where it's going.

By combining real-time media signals with environmental data and historical case burden, NTD Sentinel flags elevated outbreak risk 2–4 weeks before cases reach official reporting channels.
